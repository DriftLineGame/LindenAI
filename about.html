Security first

“We take privacy seriously”
means nothing on its own.

So here’s exactly what SCODE AI does, and the one thing still being built.

Live

Your data stays in BC

SCODE AI runs on our own computer in Campbell River. No outside AI company sees your chats. We never sell your information, show ads or track you.

Live

Locked down

Passwords are scrambled with scrypt, so even we can’t read them. Every connection is encrypted, sign-ins are rate-limited, and account files are never made public.

Live

You’re in control

Turn Memory off, delete any memory or chat, or delete your account. Deleting your account wipes your chats, memories and picture right away.

Live

Honest about training

If “Help improve SCODE AI” is on, copies of your messages may help train future SCODE models, with your name, email and account removed first. One switch turns it off.

Live

Made for students, 16+

Accounts are for ages 16 and up. No adult content, two safety checks on every picture, and a points budget so nobody can take over the server.

The SCODE story

Made in Campbell River.

SCODE AI started as a question: what if an AI didn’t have to live in a giant data center owned by a giant company?

So LJ built one at home. A laptop, free open models, a lot of late nights, and a website anyone can open. It grew from one chat box into eight modes, accounts, memory and live local info like BC Ferries times.

“Friendly, but I’ll tell you the truth instead of just flattering you.”

That’s how Linden, the AI you talk to, describes itself. SCODE is the brand behind it: the mountain is a nod to home on the coast of BC, where every project here is made.
