Privacy Policy

SCODE AI · Effective October 9, 2026 · Version 3
We've updated our Privacy Policy and added rules for using SCODE AI. Messages are now checked automatically for harmful or illegal content, and flagged messages are reviewed by our safety team (see “Safety checks” and “Rules for using SCODE AI”). Please read it, then confirm the two boxes at the bottom to keep using your account.

SCODE AI is a small, independent project run from Campbell River, British Columbia, Canada. This page explains, in plain words, what information we collect, who can see it, and what we promise. Questions? Email scodeai2026@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect as little as we can: your name, email and a scrambled password if you make an account.
  • If Save chats is on, your chats are saved to your account on our computer, so you can see them on any device. You can delete them anytime.
  • Memory: SCODE AI remembers things you ask it to, and you can turn it off or delete them on your Account page.
  • If “Help improve SCODE AI” is on, copies of your messages, with your name, email and account removed, may be used to train future SCODE AI models. You can turn it off.
  • The AI runs on our own computer. We don't send your messages to big AI companies.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing your information. Ever.
  • You must be 16 or older to make an account.
  • Every message is checked automatically for harmful or illegal content. Flagged messages are reviewed by our safety team, and serious ones can pause or ban an account.
  • You can delete your account anytime.

Who can use SCODE AI

You must be 16 or older to make an account. Guests can try General Mini without an account, and we don't ask guests for any personal details.

Why 16? Canadian privacy law says people have to really understand what they're agreeing to. Privacy regulators say kids under 13 can't do that on their own, Quebec requires a parent's permission under 14, and Europe uses 16 as its default age. 16 clears all of those without collecting parents' details. We don't use 18 because SCODE AI has no payments, no ads, no public profiles and no way to message other people, and Image Gen has safety filters. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 16, we'll delete it.

What we collect

Your account
Your name, email address, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password, when you made the account, and when you agreed to this policy and confirmed your age. We can't see your real password.
Staying signed in
A random sign-in key saved in your browser. Our server keeps a scrambled copy so it knows it's you. It expires after 90 days of not using SCODE AI.
Email codes
The 6-digit codes we email you. They're deleted or stop working after 15 minutes.
Your messages and photos
Sent to our computer so the AI can answer. Guests' messages are used for the answer and then not saved.
Your saved chats
If you have an account and Save chats is on (Settings), your chats, including photos and pictures you make, are saved to your account on our computer and in your browser, so they show up on every device you sign in on. They're kept until you delete them, turn off Save chats, or delete your account.
Replies in progress
While SCODE AI is writing a reply, our computer keeps a copy, so the reply still finishes if you close the page and is waiting when you come back. These copies are deleted after 30 days, or sooner if you delete the chat.
Memory
Things you ask SCODE AI to remember (like “Remember that I'm in Grade 10”), plus your first name, are added to your messages so answers fit you better. You can see, add and delete them, or turn Memory off, on your Account page.
Profile picture
If you add one, a small copy is saved to your account so it shows on all your devices. You can remove it anytime.
Training copies
If “Help improve SCODE AI” is on (it starts on for accounts), we save a copy of each message you send and the reply you get, to train and test future SCODE AI models. Before it's saved, the copy is separated from your account: no name, email, account number or exact time is kept, and email addresses and phone numbers in the text are blanked out. Photos aren't kept. Because these copies aren't linked to you, we can't find or delete one person's copies later, so turn it off on your Account page if you don't want new messages included. Guests' messages are never used for training.
Points
How many points you've used, counted per account (or, for guests, per internet address). This is only kept in memory and is wiped whenever our server restarts.
Weather location
If you tap “Use my location,” your browser sends your location once to find the closest town. It isn't saved. The town you pick is remembered only on your device.
Ferry location
Only if you turn on location in SCODE Ferries: your rough location (to about 100 metres) is sent with your ferry questions so we can work out your drive to the terminal. It isn't saved on our server, and you can turn it off any time.
Safety checks
Every message, and the replies to accounts, is checked by software on our own computer (a safety model and a word list) for harmful or illegal content. Nothing is sent to another company for this.
Flagged messages
If a message is flagged, a copy of that chat (the last few messages, never photos) is saved for review by the SCODE safety team: the person who runs SCODE AI and our lead security and safety manager. The flagged chat is removed from your account. Records of warnings and cleared flags are deleted after 30 days; flags that led to a ban are kept for the length of the ban plus one year; records the law requires us to keep are kept as long as the law says. Deleting your account doesn’t remove a ban or its record while the ban lasts.
Safety links
To stop banned people from making new accounts, when you use SCODE AI with an account our computer saves a scrambled code for the internet connection you're on and a random ID that your browser makes for SCODE AI. The scrambled code can't be turned back into your internet address. These are only used by the safety team to spot accounts made to get around a ban, and new accounts can't be made from a browser a banned account used. They're deleted 6 months after they were last used, or when you delete your account (unless the account is banned). Temporary or throwaway email addresses can't be used for accounts.
Essay Coach
When you check your writing, it's sent to our computer to get feedback and is checked by SCODE Safety. It's never used for training. While a check runs, our computer keeps a copy of the feedback so it finishes even if you close the page (see Replies in progress). If chat history is on, each essay and its feedback is saved to your account in Recents, like a chat, so you can come back to it on any device; delete it there any time. With chat history off, your latest essay is kept only in your browser.
Pictures you make
Made on our computer and sent to you. They're saved with your chat if Save chats is on, and otherwise not saved.
Reports and emails
If you email us (like with Report issue), we get your email address and what you wrote, in our Gmail inbox.

We don't use cookies for tracking, ads, or analytics. The site only stores things in your browser that make it work (like your sign-in, your settings, a copy of your saved chats and the random browser ID used for safety).

Who can see it

  • The SCODE team: only the person who runs SCODE AI can open the files on our computer (accounts, saved chats and memories), and only to keep the service running, fix a problem, or answer your requests. We don't read your saved chats, except a chat that was flagged by our safety checks, which the safety team reviews. Training copies (which aren't linked to anyone) may be reviewed to build and check training data. When checking a flag or a possible ban evasion, the safety team can also see account details (name, email, when it was made and its safety links).
  • Services that help run the site: GitHub hosts the website, Cloudflare connects your browser to our computer, Google Fonts provides the lettering, and Google’s Gmail sends code emails. Like any website, they can see your internet address when you connect.
  • When you use a mode: Search sends your search words to a search engine (DuckDuckGo). Link visits the page you pasted. Weather asks Environment Canada for a town’s forecast (not your exact location). Ferries asks BC Ferries for sailing times (not your location), and its map loads from Leaflet and Esri's map pictures, which see the map area being shown. None of these get your name or email.
  • The law: we'd only share information if Canadian law truly required it, and only what's required.

We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we never use it for ads.

Where it's kept and for how long

Everything is stored on the SCODE computer in British Columbia, Canada. We keep your account, saved chats, memories and picture until you delete them or your account. If you delete your account, your name, email, password, sign-ins, saved chats, memories and picture are removed right away. Training copies aren't linked to your account, so they stay.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are scrambled with a strong one-way method (scrypt).
  • Connections to SCODE AI are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The account, chat and training files are never uploaded or made public, and sign-ins are rate-limited to stop guessing.

No system is perfect. If there's ever a breach that could harm you, we'll email you and report it to the privacy regulator as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account anytime from the account menu (tap your initial, then Delete account).
  • See or fix your information: email us and we'll send you what we have or correct it, within 30 days.
  • Clear your chats by deleting them, or by turning off Save chats in Settings (that deletes them from every device and our computer).
  • Memory: turn it off, or delete one memory or all of them, on your Account page (tap your picture, then Account).
  • Training: turn off “Help improve SCODE AI” on your Account page and new messages won't be used.
  • Take back your agreement by deleting your account.

If you're not happy with how we handled something, you can complain to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (oipc.bc.ca).

Rules for using SCODE AI

  • Don’t use SCODE AI to get help hurting anyone, making weapons, making or buying illegal drugs, or committing crimes, including by pretending it’s a story, a game or a test.
  • Never send sexual content involving anyone under 18.
  • Messages are checked automatically. A blocked message shows a warning; after 3 warnings in 30 days, the next one pauses your account. Serious messages pause your account right away while it’s reviewed, usually within 24 hours.
  • After a review, we may restore your account, suspend it for a day, a week, a month or a year, or ban it permanently, and block that email from signing up again. We email you the decision and the reason.
  • Don’t make a new account to get around a pause or ban. Accounts made to get around a ban are banned too, and we may stop new accounts from being made on that browser or internet connection.
  • You can ask for another look at any decision by replying to our email within 14 days.
  • If you’re going through a hard time, SCODE AI will never ban you for saying so. It will point you to people who can help, like 9-8-8.

When we share information with authorities

We report child sexual abuse material to Cybertip.ca and the police, as Canadian law requires. If a message makes us believe a child is being harmed, we report it to child protection services, as BC law requires. We may also share information with police when we believe someone is in danger, when it helps a lawful investigation, or when a court orders it. We share only what’s needed, and we never share your information for any other reason.

Using the AI safely

  • AI can be wrong. Double-check anything important, and never use SCODE AI in an emergency.
  • Don't type private things like passwords, ID numbers, bank details or health details.
  • Don't use SCODE AI for anything illegal, harmful or hateful. Image Gen blocks unsafe requests and pictures. We may block accounts that break these rules.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and ask you to read and agree again before you keep using your account.
