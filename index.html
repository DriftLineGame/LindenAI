Privacy Policy

SCODE AI · Effective October 3, 2026 · Version 1
We've added a Privacy Policy. Please read it, then confirm the two boxes at the bottom to keep using your account.

SCODE AI is a small, independent project run from Campbell River, British Columbia, Canada. This page explains, in plain words, what information we collect, who can see it, and what we promise. Questions? Email scodeai2026@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We collect as little as we can: your name, email and a scrambled password if you make an account.
  • Your chats are saved only in your own browser, not on our server.
  • The AI runs on our own computer. We don't send your messages to big AI companies.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling or sharing your information. Ever.
  • You must be 16 or older to make an account.
  • You can delete your account anytime.

Who can use SCODE AI

You must be 16 or older to make an account. Guests can try General Mini without an account, and we don't ask guests for any personal details.

Why 16? Canadian privacy law says people have to really understand what they're agreeing to. Privacy regulators say kids under 13 can't do that on their own, Quebec requires a parent's permission under 14, and Europe uses 16 as its default age. 16 clears all of those without collecting parents' details. We don't use 18 because SCODE AI has no payments, no ads, no public profiles and no way to message other people, and Image Gen has safety filters. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 16, we'll delete it.

What we collect

Your account
Your name, email address, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password, when you made the account, and when you agreed to this policy and confirmed your age. We can't see your real password.
Staying signed in
A random sign-in key saved in your browser. Our server keeps a scrambled copy so it knows it's you. It expires after 90 days of not using SCODE AI.
Email codes
The 6-digit codes we email you. They're deleted or stop working after 15 minutes.
Your messages and photos
Sent to our computer so the AI can answer. They're used for that answer and then not saved on our server. Nobody reads them.
Your chat history
Saved only in your browser on your device (if Save chats is on in Settings). We can't see it. Turning it off, or clearing your browser, deletes it.
Points
How many points you've used, counted per account (or, for guests, per internet address). This is only kept in memory and is wiped whenever our server restarts.
Weather location
If you tap “Use my location,” your browser sends your location once to find the closest town. It isn't saved. The town you pick is remembered only on your device.
Ferry location
Only if you turn on location in SCODE Ferries: your rough location (to about 100 metres) is sent with your ferry questions so we can work out your drive to the terminal. It isn't saved on our server, and you can turn it off any time.
Pictures you make
Made on our computer and sent to you. They're not saved on our server.
Reports and emails
If you email us (like with Report issue), we get your email address and what you wrote, in our Gmail inbox.

We don't use cookies for tracking, ads, or analytics. The site only stores things in your browser that make it work (like your sign-in, your settings and your saved chats).

Who can see it

  • The SCODE team: only the person who runs SCODE AI can open the account file, and only to keep the service running or answer your requests.
  • Services that help run the site: GitHub hosts the website, Cloudflare connects your browser to our computer, Google Fonts provides the lettering, and Google’s Gmail sends code emails. Like any website, they can see your internet address when you connect.
  • When you use a mode: Search sends your search words to a search engine (DuckDuckGo). Link visits the page you pasted. Weather asks Environment Canada for a town’s forecast (not your exact location). Ferries asks BC Ferries for sailing times (not your location), and its map loads from Leaflet and OpenStreetMap (or Esri as a backup), which see the map area being shown. None of these get your name or email.
  • The law: we'd only share information if Canadian law truly required it, and only what's required.

We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we never use it for ads.

Where it's kept and for how long

Account information is stored on the SCODE computer in British Columbia, Canada. We keep it until you delete your account. If you delete it, your name, email, password and sign-ins are removed right away.

How we protect it

  • Passwords are scrambled with a strong one-way method (scrypt).
  • Connections to SCODE AI are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The account file is never uploaded or made public, and sign-ins are rate-limited to stop guessing.

No system is perfect. If there's ever a breach that could harm you, we'll email you and report it to the privacy regulator as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account anytime from the account menu (tap your initial, then Delete account).
  • See or fix your information: email us and we'll send you what we have or correct it, within 30 days.
  • Clear your chats by deleting them, or by turning off Save chats in Settings.
  • Take back your agreement by deleting your account.

If you're not happy with how we handled something, you can complain to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (oipc.bc.ca).

Using the AI safely

  • AI can be wrong. Double-check anything important, and never use SCODE AI in an emergency.
  • Don't type private things like passwords, ID numbers, bank details or health details.
  • Don't use SCODE AI for anything illegal, harmful or hateful. Image Gen blocks unsafe requests and pictures. We may block accounts that break these rules.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and ask you to read and agree again before you keep using your account.
